Frequently Asked Questions

How does DEARLY work?

Choose a photo, tell us what you want to say, and DEARLY turns it into a beautiful, personalized message card for someone you love.

Can I use my own words?

Yes! Type anything you want, or tap "I don't know what to say" and DEARLY will give you warm, touching ideas to choose from.

How do I share to WhatsApp?

Once your message is ready, tap the green "Send on WhatsApp" button. Your card will download and open directly in WhatsApp.

Does DEARLY use Gemini AI?

Yes! If you add a Gemini API key in Settings, DEARLY uses Google's Gemini AI to craft deeply personalized messages. Without it, our built-in engine still creates beautiful results.
